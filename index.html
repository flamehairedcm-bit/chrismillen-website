How to ask

After you refer

Your patient, looked after together.

You hear from me at each stage, and your patient comes back to you.

  1. You refer

    Through the practice's referral portal or by letter. Patients can also refer themselves.

  2. The practice books your patient

    The practice manager checks the referral reaches the right specialist. Our referral coordinator contacts your patient, explains the process and cost, and books the appointment. You receive confirmation.

  3. First visit

    I take a history, examine, and discuss your patient's priorities. I then write to you with my initial thoughts.

  4. Planning visit

    I take the records needed for a diagnosis and plan: photographs, an intraoral scan, radiographs and CBCT as required, and a six-point pocket chart. You receive a copy of my report, with findings, diagnoses, treatment options and costs.

  5. Shared care

    I encourage your patient to discuss the plan with you, and to share care between us where that's appropriate. Their choice is always respected.

  6. Back to you

    I don't take on general patients. After treatment, I'll write to you with a summary of what's been provided and carry out reviews at appropriate time points, but your patient returns to you.

I'm happy to discuss any case.
